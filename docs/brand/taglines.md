# Stello taglines

Working list of tagline ideas, saved Nov 28 2026 to refine, combine or shortlist later.

## Direction (Arjun, Nov 28 2026)

- The "Storage vs thinking" and "Short and punchy" lines sound good.
- Avoid generic, cliché star/constellation talk about items.
- "Think in constellations" is great because it talks about what Stello enables, not about the items.

## Status

- **Favourite:** Think in constellations.
- **Suggested runner-up / sub-line:** See the pattern, not the pile.

## Refined list

### Like "Think in constellations"

- Think in constellations.
- See the pattern, not the pile.
- Where saves start making sense.
- Connect what caught your eye.
- From saved to understood.

### Storage vs thinking

- Not storage. A space for stellar thoughts.
- Not an archive. A place to think.
- Less keeping, more connecting.
- Bookmarks go to sleep. Stello wakes them up.
- Don't just keep it. Make something of it.

### Short and punchy

- Save. Connect. Think.
- Collect to connect.
- Saved, not forgotten.
